Asset Information

Tokens representing tangible assets like real estate, gold, or company shares. Often requires legal backing.

  • Real Estate: Fractional ownership of property.
  • Commodities: Gold, silver, oil, tokenized to represent units of the physical asset.
  • Art & Collectibles: Provenance and ownership for high-value physical items.
  • Company Shares: Digital representation of equity in a business, potentially regulated as securities.
  • Debt Instruments: Bonds or loans represented by tokens.

Tokens purely existing in the digital realm, often for use in software or virtual environments.

  • In-Game Items: Exclusive virtual items, weapons, or land within games.
  • Software Licenses: Tokens that grant usage rights to software.
  • Digital Art (NFTs): Unique, non-fungible tokens for digital creations.
  • Access Tokens: Grants access to premium digital content or communities.

Tokens used for verifiable records, voting, or loyalty programs.

  • Voting Rights: Tokens granting the ability to vote on proposals or corporate decisions.
  • Loyalty Points: Rewards or points for customer loyalty programs.
  • Certificates: Digital diplomas, licenses, or authenticity certificates.
